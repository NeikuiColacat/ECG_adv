Input And Acceleration Policy

Input is minimal_resample, 500Hz, 5000 samples = 10s. EfficientNet1DV2 uses per-sample global z-score. Benchmark backbones use dataset-level train mean/std to match ecg_ptbxl_benchmarking. PTB-XL arrays are loaded into RAM when practical. PN2021 500Hz evaluation keeps mmap caches on disk, then copies one center at a time into RAM for inference. Safe acceleration defaults are bf16 AMP where appropriate, TF32, CuDNN benchmark, fused AdamW, pinned memory, persistent workers, and prefetching.

Interpretation

The most paper-safe claim is that a PTB-XL-only 500Hz VAE latent space can act as an on-manifold online adversarial regularizer for 500Hz EfficientNet1DV2 Super5 cross-center adaptation. Direct ECGTwin/DiT synthetic samples should remain an ablation, while ECGFounder is a strong foundation-model comparison and direct K500 adaptation control.
